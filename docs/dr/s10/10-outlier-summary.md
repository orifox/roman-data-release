# 10.10 Outlier summary

Investigate outliers and provide guidance. E.g. what fraction of the 100 "reddest" objects in the survey are real? Can you filter to improve this? What fraction of the 100 "largest" or "smallest" or "flattest" galaxies at some faint magnitude are real? Can you improve by filtering? Science use cases can guide what to check -- e.g. LRDs, AGNs, emission-line galaxies, LSB galaxies, etc.
